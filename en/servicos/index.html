01 / PROCESS DISCOVERY

Clarity to choose the next step.

For small and medium businesses that feel the friction but need help defining the solution. Discovery is a standalone engagement: you receive material you can use with me, your team or another provider.

  1. Interviews and an understanding of the operation.
  2. Current process map (AS-IS), bottlenecks, rework and waste.
  3. Opportunities and a future process design (TO-BE).
  4. Prioritization matrix, recommendations and a roadmap.

02 / PRIORITIZATION

Your decision. Clarity we build together.

The matrix considers impact, effort, urgency, risk, cost, dependencies, time saved and potential return. Your company’s strategic priorities shape the final decision.

My role is to organize the options and their trade-offs so you can make a better decision without becoming dependent on a provider.

03 / OPTIONAL IMPLEMENTATION

Then, build.

After understanding, questioning and simplifying the process, implementation can include automation, system integration, pipelines, applications and infrastructure. Technical choices follow productivity, reliability and growth needs.

The goal is to reduce repetitive work and give people time to analyze, decide and solve problems. The solution evolves incrementally through measurement and learning.

What is within my scope?

Processes, data, automation and technology. If discovery reveals issues in culture, HR, legal, accounting or other specialist disciplines, I recommend involving the appropriate professional.
