About

A business perspective. An engineering mindset.

My background in Business Administration, software development and data taught me to look beyond code: understand operations, challenge processes and build with purpose.

01 / THE CONNECTION

Technology is one part of the answer.

Business Administration gave me a perspective on processes, efficiency and decision-making. Operational experience reinforced the value of understanding how work actually flows, including exceptions and dependencies.

At BNDES, I started with Java backend development and then moved deeper into software quality with TDD, BDD, Cucumber, Cypress and Gherkin. At Uello, my path through BI and analytics evolved into Data Engineering, bridging the needs of data consumers and the challenges of maintaining the platform.

My work involves breaking down problems, exploring alternatives and establishing an initial technical direction. Explaining decisions, documenting, reviewing and pairing help other people continue the implementation.
